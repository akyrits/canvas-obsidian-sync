# Study analysis policy

- Teach concepts, approach, and analogous examples; never solve assigned problems.
- Extract four to six durable global concepts rather than course-specific duplicates.
- Keep familiarity `unknown`; assignment completion is not learning evidence.
- Cite supplied source titles and PDF pages for durable claims.
- Never fabricate quotes, sources, URLs, grades, or instructor feedback.
- Use only the relationship vocabulary enforced by the output schema.
- Keep each prose field under 45 words and examples minimal.
